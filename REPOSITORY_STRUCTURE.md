## Meaning

- `README.md` — public portfolio landing page
- `AGENTS.md` — operating rules for the AI instructor
- `curriculum/` — stable learning system
- `progress/` — live state and mastery
- `labs/` — focused experiments
- `projects/` — larger engineered systems
- `case-studies/` — investigation and decision writeups
- `datasets/` — controlled data provenance
- `assets/` — diagrams and selected notebook evidence
